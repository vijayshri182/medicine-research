# Medicine Research

## Purpose
This repository documents structured, evidence-based research into Type 2 diabetes (T2D) and related metabolic dysfunction, with emphasis on distinguishing remission, disease modification, and cure. The goal is a traceable, transparent research knowledge base—not a clinical decision tool.

## Scope
- **Primary focus:** Type 2 diabetes (see `research/diseases/type-2-diabetes/`).
- **Approach:** Evidence appraisal across human trials, systematic reviews, observational studies, animal/laboratory research, and emerging interventions.
- **Boundaries:** Documentation only. No clinical advice. No automated medical decision systems. No promises of cures.

## Disease Coverage (current)
- Type 2 Diabetes (`research/diseases/type-2-diabetes/`) — biology, remission evidence, beta-cell recovery, emerging treatments, natural compounds, clinical trials, research gaps.

## Research Workflow
1. **Source management:** Register sources in `research/source-register.csv`. Appraise via `templates/study-appraisal.md`.
2. **Claim tracking:** Log material claims in `research/claim-ledger.csv` with evidence level, supporting/conflicting sources, limitations, confidence.
3. **Disease pages:** Build per-disease documentation following the roadmap in `PROJECT_PLAN.md`.
4. **Governance:** Follow `AGENTS.md` and `research/evidence-methodology.md` for safeguards and standards.

- **Phase:** Phase 0 — Initialization (complete after initial commit)

## Important Notes
- Remission ≠ cure. On-treatment benefits differ from medication-free remission/durable disease modification.
- All material claims require traceable sources; uncertainties and null results are reported.
- This repo must not contain PHI/PII, credentials, or medical advice. Follow all safeguards in `AGENTS.md`.
