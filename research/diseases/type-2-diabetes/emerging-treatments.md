# Type 2 Diabetes — Emerging Treatments

**Status:** Placeholder — Phase 3.  
**Policy:** Distinguish on-treatment benefits vs medication-free remission/durable disease modification.

## Categories
- **Incretin-based therapies:** GLP-1RAs, dual/triple agonists — report effects while on treatment and any remission data
- **Other pharmacologic combinations:** Evidence graded
- **Metabolic surgery:** Bariatric/metabolic surgery — remission rates, durability, relapse, safety
- **Regenerative approaches:** Very limited human evidence; treat as experimental/hypothesis-generating

## Documentation Requirements
For each intervention: study type, population, on-treatment vs off-treatment, remission criteria, durability/relapse, safety, limitations, human evidence strength.
