# Type 2 Diabetes — Research Gaps & Prioritization

**Status:** Placeholder — Phase 6.  
**Policy:** Prioritize by evidence strength, not plausibility alone. Map uncertainty.

## Known Research Gaps (to be refined)
- **Remission durability:** Long-term (>5 years) medication-free remission data across populations
- **Predictors:** Reliable predictors of sustained remission/relapse
- **Beta-cell recovery:** Human evidence separating recovery from compensation
- **Disease modification:** Interventions demonstrating durable pathophysiologic change
- **Natural compounds:** Robust human RCTs with safety data
- **Diversity:** Evidence in underrepresented populations

## Conservative Prioritization Framework
Score criteria (1-5, with rationale):

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Human evidence strength | 0.25 | Systematic reviews/RCTs > observational > preclinical |
| Likely benefit (clinical significance) | 0.20 | Meaningful outcomes, not just surrogates |
| Durability | 0.15 | Sustained effect/medication-free remission |
| Safety | 0.15 | Known harms/low risk of serious harm |
| Feasibility/affordability | 0.10 | Practical for research/access considerations |
| Remaining uncertainty | 0.15 | Inverse or explicit uncertainty penalty |

**Rule:** Plausibility cannot override weak human evidence. Do not rank interventions as "proven reversals". Report confidence and uncertainties for each priority.
