# Type 2 Diabetes — Remission Evidence

**Status:** Placeholder — Phase 1 priority.  
**Policy:** Distinguish remission from cure/control; report durability, relapse, safety, limitations.

## Definition (to be finalized)
Document accepted definitions (e.g., DiRECT/DIADEM-style) noting:
- Glycemic criteria (e.g., HbA1c thresholds) and whether achieved off glucose-lowering medications
- Duration of remission required
- Study-specific variations

## Key Trials to Verify
- **DiRECT** (Diabetes Remission Clinical Trial) — primary publications, follow-up, relapse, limitations
- **DIADEM-I** — primary publications, design/population, outcomes, limitations
- Relevant systematic reviews/meta-analyses

## What to Record Per Trial
- Design, setting, population (inclusion/exclusion), sample size
- Intervention (diet/weight loss etc.)
- Remission criteria, rates, time to remission
- Durability/follow-up, relapse
- Safety/adverse events
- Generalizability/limitations
- Conflicts of interest

## Evidence Gaps
- Long-term durability beyond trial follow-ups
- Predictors of sustained remission
- Relapse mechanisms
- Applicability to diverse populations
