**Policy:** Never claim recruiting without verifying current registry status. Record verification dates.

## Reproducible Method
1. **Search registries:** ClinicalTrials.gov (primary), WHO ICTRP, CTRI (India) as relevant
2. **Verify status:** Check current recruitment/status on registry page (not just secondary sources)
3. **Record:** NCT/registry ID, title, sponsor, design, population/criteria, interventions/comparators, endpoints, start/completion, status, results posted?, location
4. **Track verification:** `last-verified` date for each entry
5. **Update:** Re-verify periodically; mark stale if not verified recently
